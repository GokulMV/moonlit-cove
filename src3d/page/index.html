Life

Fireflies all along the forest edge after dark, moths at the lamps, midges at dusk, butterflies and dragonflies by day; fish leaping and shoaling in the shallows; gulls over the surf, sandpipers chasing the waves, bats and ghost crabs at night, now and then dolphins offshore; what the tide leaves on the sand.
